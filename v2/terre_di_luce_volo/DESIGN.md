## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the synthesis of precision aerial technology and the sensory, pastoral beauty of viticulture in the Oltrepò Pavese. Moving radically away from cold, militaristic drone interfaces, dark HUD aesthetics, and clinical surveying tools, the visual narrative is sunny, warm, organic, and deeply human.

The interface draws heavy inspiration from refined bio-dynamic winery estates: sunlit limestone, lush canopy greens, terracottas dried by afternoon wind, and expansive rolling horizons. The visual movement pairs architectural poise with tactile gentleness. 

- **Aesthetic Direction:** Solar-Organic Elegance. Generous breathing room, warm ambient luminosity, sculptural silhouettes, and gentle curvilinear compositions that echo winding vineyard slopes seen from above.
- **Target Audience:** Winemakers, agronomists, geotechnical professionals, and historic estate curators who demand contemporary precision without sacrificing cultural authenticity or warmth.
- **Emotional Signature:** Inviting, grounded, luminous, and refined.

## Colors

The palette is tuned exclusively for a radiant light-mode experience rooted in natural landscape pigments:

- **Primary (`#71B344` - Vineyard Leaf):** Bright, photosynthetic, life-giving green. Used for structural actions, active indicators, and core agricultural signals.
- **Secondary (`#E6503A` - Terracotta Coral):** Earthy clay fired with sunlight. Reserved for high-priority points of conversion, highlight badges, and precision pinpoints on aerial layers.
- **Tertiary (`#FFD23F` - Warm Solar Amber):** Golden hour warmth. Used sparingly for energetic accents, sun-exposure indicators, and luminous decorative highlights.
- **Accent Sky (`#335CA7` - Deep Sky):** Open atmospheric contrast. Employed for technical telemetry labels, survey layer overlays, and water index metrics.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FBF9F5` (Ivory Alabaster, crisp yet warmed).
  - Raised Surface / Card: `#F3EFE6` (Fine Silt & Chalk).
  - Elevated Popout: `#FFFFFF` (Pure sunlight highlight).
- **Text & Neutral (`#1E2229` - Warm Charcoal Anthracite):** Deep basalt tone with warm undertones; prevents harsh synthetic contrasts and pairs effortlessly with editorial serif headlines. Muted body text shifts to `#545B66`.

## Typography

The typographical treatment balances literary grandeur with aerodynamic clarity:

- **Playfair Display:** Chosen for all prominent headlines and expressive callouts. Its high stroke contrast, elegant serifs, and organic curves create an authentic, slow-paced Mediterranean editorial rhythm reminiscent of vintage estate reserves and architectural monographs.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Governs all body copy, interface controls, dynamic charts, and geospatial captions. The geometric yet soft counters ensure effortless legibility even when rendering complex agronomic metrics or drone telemetry.
- **Stylistic Conventions:**
  - Headlines use negative letter tracking to retain tension and intimacy.
  - Subheaders and numerical metadata leverage uppercase styling with expanded letter tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.03em` to `0.06em`).
  - Paragraphs maintain generous leading (1.6x) to preserve sunlight and open space between lines.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout draws inspiration from panoramic vineyard vistas and open horizons:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum horizontal margin of 48px (`3rem`). Content bounds cap gracefully at a maximum width of 1320px for editorial reading comfort.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical rhythm is prioritized. Section breaks breathe through 80px to 120px margins, mimicking open sky between hilltops. Content groups cluster together using `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) to retain compositional tension.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows in favor of ambient daylight and gentle tonal stacking:

- **Tonal Layers:** The primary depth mechanic is subtle contrast between `#FBF9F5` (background) and `#F3EFE6` (card containers). Layering pure `#FFFFFF` over `#F3EFE6` produces an organic sensation of sun striking a warm terracotta or limestone surface.
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** When floating elements require separation (such as flight booking widgets, interactive NDVI filters, or floating navigation headers), shadows employ warm amber-grey pigments rather than synthetic black:
  - *Sunlit Float:* `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(78, 68, 52, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(78, 68, 52, 0.04)`.
  - *Gentle Hover:* `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(78, 68, 52, 0.12)`.
- **Low-Contrast Micro-Borders:** To keep shapes crisp under intense natural light without introducing rigid wireframes, elements may feature a gossamer border: `1px solid rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is built on continuous, voluptuous curves inspired by terraced hillsides, grape leaves, and drone aerodynamic surfaces:

- **24px (`1.5rem`) Master Radius:** All primary cards, interactive media containers, modal sheets, and showcase viewports adhere strictly to a 24px corner radius.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Used universally for action buttons, filter tags, chip indicators, and interactive toggles to preserve a soft, inviting touch.
- **Organic Cutouts:** Feature cards and aerial visual frames may leverage asymmetric or soft parabolic corner masking to evoke natural undulating terrain.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta Coral (`#E6503A`) solid fill with white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), horizontal padding `space-xl`, vertical padding `space-md`. Hover state gently deepens to `#D3402C` with a warm ambient lift.
- **Secondary:** Vineyard Leaf (`#71B344`) solid fill or outlined in a 1.5px leaf border against `#F3EFE6`. Provides an organic, grounding action.
- **Ghost / Editorial:** Anthracite text (`#1E2229`) with an organic underline that swells on hover; accompanied by a subtle trailing circular arrow.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill silhouettes with 8px vertical and 16px horizontal padding.
- Inactive: `#F3EFE6` surface with warm charcoal text (`#1E2229`).
- Active: Vineyard Leaf (`#71B344`) or Sky Blue (`#335CA7`) fill with crisp white text, accompanied by an optional micro-dot indicator.

### Cards
- Bound by the canonical `24px` radius.
- Base background: `#F3EFE6`.
- Internal padding: `space-lg` (24px) up to `space-xl` (40px) for editorial showcases.
- Framing: Subtle border `rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.05)`, removing all heavy dividers.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 56px, pill or 24px radius, tinted with `#FFFFFF` over the ivory background. Border: 1px subtle sand-grey (`#E5DFC8`). Active focus shifts to a 1.5px ring of Vineyard Leaf (`#71B344`) with zero abrasive blue glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved squircle forms (8px radius for checkboxes, circles for radios). Checked states illuminate with Vineyard Leaf (`#71B344`) featuring an ivory check mark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Orthophoto & NDVI Viewer:** Framed within a continuous 24px bezel. Overlay controls (zoom, multispectral layer toggle) float on translucent ivory frost (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) using pill-shaped buttons.
- **Territory Metric Badges:** Floating stats (e.g., "Pendenza: 32%", "Vigore Vegetativo: Ottimale") housed in miniature cards with 16px radius, solar yellow or sky blue accent icons, and refined `label-md` typography.
